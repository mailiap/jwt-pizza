# Incident: 2025-04-08 1:21:31 MT

## Summary

The JWT Headquarters Pizza Factory failed to create pizzas for a period of 5 minutes. This impacted diners’ ability to place orders, causing a temporary service problem. The error was caused by a failure in the factory service. The issue was diagnosed and reported quickly, and functionality was fully restored after the chaos was resolved.

## Detection

This incident was detected when no alert was triggered, and no team or person was paged. Instead, the issue was discovered manually when I checked my logs on Grafana and noticed something was wrong. Because there was no automated alerting, the response was delayed until I manually investigated the logs.

To improve this, alerting will be set up by the team so that similar failures are detected automatically, allowing for faster response times and reduce relying on manual checks.

## Impact

For about 20 minutes between 1:31 MT and 1:51 MT on 04/08/25, the JWT Headquarters Pizza Factory was unable to create pizzas due to a failure in the factory service. 

This incident affected all customers attempting to order pizzas during that time.

No support tickets or social media posts were submitted, as the issue was detected through manual log checks rather than user reports.
